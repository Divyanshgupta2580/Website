# Image Sources & Attribution Registry — GG Construction Co.

This registry documents all photographic assets used on the GG Construction Co. website in compliance with licensing requirements, ethical presentation standards, and anti-misrepresentation guidelines.

## Policy & Usage Declaration

- **No Competitor Images**: No images from competitor websites (such as Brick & Bolt, Pahwa Builders, GSA Buildwell, Adani, or other builders/aggregators) are used anywhere on this website.
- **License Basis**: All external photographic assets are sourced from **Unsplash** under the **Unsplash License**, granting free commercial and non-commercial usage without mandatory attribution (though credited below for full transparency).
- **Representative Presentation**: All images of completed structures or ongoing site works are classified as **Representative Construction Imagery** depicting structural systems, low-rise residential typologies, RCC framing, and construction processes typical of the Delhi NCR region (Rohini, Pitampura, and surrounding localities). None of these photographs are represented or claimed as proprietary, completed projects of GG Construction Co.

## Verification Statement

All imagery included in this codebase has been verified against the strict requirements of GG Construction Co.:
1. **No foreign skyscraper, ultra-luxury high-rise, or mega-infrastructure imagery** (no suspension bridges, mega-cranes, or 50-storey towers).
2. **Indian low-rise construction context**: Imagery focuses on low-rise residential buildings (up to 4–5 floors), brick/mortar work, RCC casting, rebar tie-ups, shopfronts, and practical site supervision appropriate for Delhi municipal contexts.
3. **No deceptive claims**: Clear representative labels accompany project presentations.
